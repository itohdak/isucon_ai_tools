## Change (commit 6ca111f)

One `DELETE FROM livecomments WHERE livestream_id=? AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM ng_words w WHERE w.livestream_id=? AND livecomments.comment LIKE CONCAT('%', w.word COLLATE utf8mb4_general_ci, '%'))` instead of a SELECT of the NG words and one DELETE per word. Verified on s2 in a rolled-back transaction: comments `hello World`, `unrelated`, `あいうえお` with NG words `WORLD` and `いう` -> 2 rows deleted (`unrelated` kept), plan uses `idx_livestream_id` on both tables, no leftover rows after ROLLBACK.
