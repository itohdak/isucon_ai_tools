## Hypothesis

`verifyUserSession`/`session.Get` decodes the same cookie (base64 + HMAC-SHA256 + gob) on nearly every request; memoising the decode of an already-verified cookie removes ~9% of the app's CPU on s1, the constrained host, without touching search latency (the pure DB round trips dominate that).

## Change (commit 79dacb7)

`webapp/go/session_cache.go` (new) wraps `*sessions.CookieStore` in `memoStore`; `main.go` passes it to `session.Middleware`. Only cookies the underlying store already verified are cached (key = exact cookie value), a hit returns a fresh `Session` with a shallow copy of `Values` (all int64/string; `loginHandler` mutates them), `Save` is delegated, 10 minute TTL, map dropped above 200k entries, `verifyUserSession` still checks `EXPIRES` against the clock every request. Smoke test with curl: register 201, login 200, three `/api/user/me` 200, garbage cookie 401.
