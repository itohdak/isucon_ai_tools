## Hypothesis
Client-side parameter interpolation removes 2 of every 3 round trips per query. Both hosts are CPU-saturated, and per-statement network/protocol overhead is exactly what saturates them (s1 `Syscall6` 30% of app CPU; s2 spends 15s/window on Prepare alone), so this should help both.

## Change
`webapp/go/main.go` `connectDB`: `conf.InterpolateParams = true`. Safe for this app: params are ints, strings and `[]byte` (icon BLOB); no `NO_BACKSLASH_ESCAPES`, utf8mb4 charset; no `time.Time` params (timestamps are int64 unix seconds).
