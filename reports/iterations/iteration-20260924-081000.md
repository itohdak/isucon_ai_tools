## Hypothesis

Iteration 3 showed the tip-producing viewer path is where latency matters. Apply the same round-trip removal to the other calls a viewer makes (and that are not search): `GET /api/livestream/:id` (5k calls/run, 44 s total), enter/exit, login, `GET /api/user/me`, `GET /api/user/:u`, theme.

## Change (commit b58c29f)

`getLivestreamHandler` uses the immutable livestream/tag caches and no transaction; `enterLivestreamHandler`/`exitLivestreamHandler` do their single INSERT/DELETE without BEGIN/COMMIT; `loginHandler`, `getMeHandler`, `getUserHandler`, `getStreamerThemeHandler` read users/themes through the existing insert-only caches (`getUserModelByName`, `getUserModelByID`, `getThemeModel`) and drop their read-only transactions. Streamer-side GETs (report, ngwords) and all list handlers untouched.
