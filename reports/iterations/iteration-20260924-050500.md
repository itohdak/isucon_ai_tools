## Iteration 14 — `GOGC=400` in `isupipe-go.service` (kept, neutral)
Hypothesis: heap is ~70MB on a 3.6GB host and GC/allocation is ~13% of app CPU; a larger GC target trades idle RAM for CPU. Change: `Environment=GOGC=400` in the tracked unit (`common/etc/systemd/system/isupipe-go.service`); verified in `/proc/<pid>/environ`. Result: 171345 / 151246 / 156011 / 160534 (avg 159784) vs 157683 (6 runs at `5ec4247`): +1.3%, within noise. Kept (no downside). Commit `c0a52ff`.

## Iteration 15 — no transaction in the hottest read-only handlers (kept, small +)
Hypothesis: `getLivecommentsHandler`, `getReactionsHandler`, `getTagHandler` (~17k requests per window) wrapped a single list statement plus immutable lookups in BEGIN/COMMIT (2 extra round trips and 2 extra statements on the saturated s2). Change: new `dbq` interface (Get/SelectContext, satisfied by `*sqlx.Tx` and `*sqlx.DB`) used by `fillLivestreamResponse`, `fillUserResponse`, `getUserModelByID`, `getThemeModel`, `fillLivecommentResponseWithLivestream`, `fillReactionResponseWithLivestream`; the three handlers call them with `dbConn` and no longer open a transaction. Safe because every follow-up read is of insert-only/immutable data (no snapshot dependency), per App Understanding's analysis. Result: 176064 / 158750 / 153660 / 160160 (avg 162159) vs 159784: +1.5%, within noise but non-negative and fewer statements. Commit `c882bfb`.

## Iteration 16 — `isudns.records(name,type)` index re-test (REJECTED, live-only, dropped)
Hypothesis: PowerDNS lookups are now ~2/3 of mysqld time on s2 (120s of ~180s per window), so the index (EXPLAIN: full scan of ~1.5k rows -> `ref`) might win now even if the benchmark's adaptive DNS attacker escalates. Result: 165615 / 140564 / 141017 / 141711 (avg ~147.2k) vs ~162k without; bench log: `DNSAttacker並列数: 9`, `名前解決成功数: 19093` (vs parallelism 3 and ~5-7k resolutions without the index). The attacker escalation is essentially unbounded and costs more (pdns on s1 + connection/thread load) than the index saves. Third rejection (session 1 iteration 6, session 2 iteration 2, now). Index dropped; baseline reconfirmed 169287 / 154106. Durable rule: do not speed up PowerDNS's DB lookups in this environment.
